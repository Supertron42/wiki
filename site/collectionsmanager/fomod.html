What a FOMOD does

A FOMOD is a mod installer that asks you to choose options. CM can capture those choices for the collection, while the matching MO2 helper performs the actual installation.

Read the status

Configured and review-needed badges are shortcuts to saved metadata, not proof of a successful installation. The actual installer checks the exact archive and available options again.

Choose or revise options

  1. Select the mod and choose the installer-options action.
  2. Work through the real installer choices and confirm them. Cancel leaves the saved setup unchanged.
  3. Review the captured choices in the collection. A changed archive or incompatible options require another review.

Rerun an installer

Use the reviewed reinstall workflow for an installed mod. Pay attention to replacement and activation choices. Skipping a manual step keeps it unfinished; it does not create a verified install.

Share useful choices

Test the exact file version and choices you export. Include clear instructions for choices that depend on other optional mods. Keep manual decisions visible rather than assuming one installer configuration fits every user.
