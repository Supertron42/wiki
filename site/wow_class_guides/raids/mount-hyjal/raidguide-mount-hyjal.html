Mount Hyjal - Role Assignment Guide

Bear and Prot Warrior own bosses; Prot Paladin owns adds/control. The deliberate final exception is Archimonde: Prot Warrior permanently tanks him because Berserker Rage is the preferred Fear answer.

Protection WarriorWave backup, dangerous single-target control, permanent Archimonde MT.
BearMain Tank for Winterchill, Anetheron, Kaz'rogal, and Azgalor.
Protection PaladinWave AoE anchor, Anetheron Infernals, Azgalor Doomguards, utility elsewhere.

Hyjal Wave Doctrine

Eight fixed waves precede each of the first four bosses. Wiping means repeating them, so this is a controlled-execution section, not a speed-run strat.

🛡 Tanks

  • Prot Paladin: main AoE anchor - Ghouls, Fel Stalkers, clustered ground packs, Giant Infernals when Fire Resistance is appropriate.
  • Bear: Abominations/heaviest physical mobs; keep Disease Cloud away and kite/stutter high Abomination counts when useful.
  • Prot Warrior: loose dangerous mobs/casters, Necromancer interrupts, backup Abomination control.

⚔ Kill / Control Priority

  1. Shadowy Necromancers - stun/interrupt Shadow Bolt and Unholy Frenzy.
  2. Fel Stalkers - protect caster/healer mana.
  3. Frost Wyrms / Gargoyles - ranged priority.
  4. Banshees - physical DPS when Anti-Magic Shell is active; decurse Banshee Curse immediately.
  5. Abominations - respect Disease Cloud and Knockdown.

✚ Healers

  • Do not stand in Abomination Disease Cloud.
  • Decurse Banshee Curse immediately.
  • Expect Abomination Knockdown / Unholy Frenzy tank spikes.
  • Call loose Fel Stalkers before healer mana is burned.

★ Wave Rule

  • No AoE before Paladin has the pack.
  • Dangerous casters are interrupted even during AoE.
  • Ranged owns flying mobs while tanks stabilize ground packs.
BOSS 1

Rage Winterchill

What Happens

  • Bear owns the boss; everyone reacts to Icebolt, Frost Nova, and Death & Decay.
  • Death & Decay deals 15% max HP per second: movement always beats uptime.
  • Frost Nova must be dispelled quickly so nobody is rooted inside Death & Decay.

🛡 Tanks

Bear - Main Tank

  • Hold Winterchill at the boss marker and face him away.
  • Move the boss directly out of Death & Decay; never soak it.
  • If rooted by Frost Nova inside danger, call Tank rooted.
  • PvP trinket/Living Action Potion is your emergency Icebolt break.

Prot Warrior - Backup / DPS

  • Do not compete for threat; emergency pickup only.

Prot Paladin - Utility

  • Keep Blessing of Freedom ready for Bear/key melee.
  • Primary tank-side Frost Nova dispel.

✚ Healers

  • Healers 1-2: Bear.
  • Healer 3: rapid-response Icebolt healer.
  • Remaining healers: raid.
  • Dispel Frost Nova immediately if it prevents movement.
  • Never finish a cast while standing in Death & Decay.

⚔ Melee DPS

  • Stay behind boss and move instantly from Death & Decay.
  • Free Action Potion is the progression consumable for Frost Armor's Chilled effect.
  • Use assigned Icebolt self-break if targeted.

➶ Ranged DPS

  • Spread around boss so Death & Decay movement does not collapse everyone together.
  • Move immediately from Death & Decay.
  • Mages may Blink/Ice Block Icebolt pressure.

★ Special Assignments

Frost Nova Dispel

  • Prot Paladin: Bear first.
  • Priest 1: melee.
  • Priest 2 / Paladin 2: ranged/healers.

Icebolt Survival

  • Progression default: PvP trinket or Living Action Potion where practical. Breaking the stun also removes the Icebolt DoT.

Wipe Conditions

  • Standing in Death & Decay.
  • Bear rooted in Death & Decay without dispel.
  • Icebolt target receives no fast heal/self-break.
  • Melee loses movement control to Chilled.

Raid-Leader Calls

“Icebolt NAME.” “Nova - dispel.” “Tank rooted.” “Death & Decay - MOVE.” “Freedom Bear.”
BOSS 2

Anetheron

What Happens

  • Bear tanks Anetheron; Fire-Resist Prot Paladin owns Towering Infernals far away.
  • Carrion Swarm reduces healing done by 75%; healer spacing is mandatory.
  • Rogue maintains Wound Poison to cut Vampiric Aura healing.

🛡 Tanks

Bear - Main Tank

  • Hold Anetheron at the boss marker and face away.
  • Keep him at least 25 yards from all Towering Infernals.
  • Do not chase add spawns.

Prot Paladin - Towering Infernal Tank

  • Wear roughly 200-250 Fire Resistance for progression.
  • Tank Infernals at the distant add marker.
  • Use ranged pickup and move every Infernal away from boss/ranged stacks.
  • Progression default: ranged kills each Infernal instead of accumulating them.

Prot Warrior - Backup / DPS

  • If Paladin is slept during a spawn, intercept/hold the Infernal until he recovers.

✚ Healers

  • Healer 1 and Healer 2 both heal Bear from opposite sides of the boss.
  • Healer 3 stays on Paladin/Infernals from the distant add area.
  • Remaining healers spread between raid sectors.
  • If a healer gets Carrion Swarm's 75% healing reduction, backup healers immediately cover them.
  • Sleep cannot be dispelled.

⚔ Melee DPS

  • Loose semicircle behind boss.
  • Rogue 1 keeps Wound Poison active the entire fight.
  • Stay on boss; do not chase Infernals unless specifically assigned.

➶ Ranged DPS

  • Use assigned ranged groups instead of one giant stack.
  • If Inferno targets you, run toward Paladin's add marker.
  • Once Paladin has control, assigned ranged hard-swaps and kills the Infernal.

★ Special Assignments

Healing Reduction

  • Rogue 1: Wound Poison primary.
  • Hunter 1: Aimed Shot backup.

Infernal Pickup

  • Hunter 1 Misdirects awkward Infernal spawns to Prot Paladin on call.

Wipe Conditions

  • Multiple healers clipped by one Carrion Swarm.
  • Infernal stays within Vampiric Aura range.
  • Loose Infernal reaches ranged/healers.
  • Healing reduction falls off.
  • Sleeping tank healer has no backup.

Raid-Leader Calls

“Carrion - spread.” “Inferno NAME - add marker.” “Paladin pickup.” “Ranged kill Infernal.” “Wound Poison check.” “Healer asleep - COVER.”
BOSS 3

Kaz'rogal

What Happens

  • Bear is Main Tank; Paladin boss-tanking is intentionally avoided because Mark punishes mana users.
  • Every mana user must enter each Mark comfortably above 3,000 mana or risk exploding within 15 yards.
  • Mark frequency accelerates, making the fight a mana soft-enrage.

🛡 Tanks

Bear - Main Tank

  • Pull Kaz'rogal into Horde NPC support, then turn him so Thrall attacks from behind and never eats Cleave.
  • Only Bear belongs in front unless Warrior is deliberately called to split Cleave.
  • Rage resource makes Bear ideal for this fight.

Prot Warrior - Backup / Cleave Assist

  • Stay behind by default. Move to the split position only on raid-leader call.

Prot Paladin - Utility, Not Boss Tank

  • Maintain Judgement of Wisdom.
  • Watch personal mana aggressively; Divine Shield may negate one Mark if needed.

✚ Healers

  • Healers 1-2: Bear.
  • Remaining healers: raid / melee War Stomp.
  • Stay comfortably above 3,000 mana before every Mark.
  • Use efficient heals and consume Mana Potions/Dark Runes early enough to matter.

⚔ Melee DPS

  • Stay behind boss.
  • Free Action Potion is useful for War Stomp.
  • Mana-using melee watches mana exactly like casters.
  • If near-empty before Mark, leave to the explosion marker.

➶ Ranged DPS

  • Maintain spacing from any low-mana player.
  • Stay comfortably above 3,000 mana before every Mark.
  • Mages may Ice Block one Mark if required.

★ Special Assignments

Mana Rule

  • Absolute minimum: enter Mark above 3,000 mana.
  • Raid safety rule: at roughly 4,000 or less with Mark approaching and no instant recovery ready, move to isolation.

Mana Support

  • Prot Paladin: Judgement of Wisdom.
  • Shadow Priests: highest-value mana groups.
  • Shamans: planned Mana Tide usage.

Wipe Conditions

  • Mana user reaches zero during Mark in raid.
  • Bear cleaves Thrall/raid.
  • Mana cooldowns are held too long.
  • Multiple low-mana players stack together.

Raid-Leader Calls

“Mark soon - CHECK MANA.” “Low mana - OUT.” “Judgement of Wisdom check.” “Stomp.” “Thrall behind.”
BOSS 4

Azgalor

What Happens

  • Bear tanks Azgalor; Prot Paladin owns Lesser Doomguards at a Doom camp over 30 yards away.
  • Doom kills its target after 20 seconds and spawns a Doomguard at the corpse.
  • Ranged/healers stay beyond 30 yards; healers pre-HoT Bear before every raid-wide silence.

🛡 Tanks

Bear - Main Tank

  • Tank Azgalor at boss marker, face away, and keep Thrall behind him.
  • During Howl, if threat is secure, stop attacking during the silence to avoid parry-haste spikes.

Prot Paladin - Lesser Doomguard Tank

  • Stand at Doom camp over 30 yards from boss.
  • Every Doomed player dies at your marker.
  • Pick up Doomguard instantly with ranged Holy threat/Consecration.
  • Progression default: Doomguard squad kills each add.

Prot Warrior - Doomguard Backup / DPS

  • If a second Doomguard overlaps, intercept and hold it at Doom camp.

✚ Healers

  • Healers 1-2: Bear.
  • Healer 3: Paladin/Doomguard camp.
  • Remaining healers: raid/melee Rain damage.
  • Ranged healers stay over 30 yards from boss.
  • Pre-HoT/shield Bear before every Howl silence.
  • Improved Concentration Aura belongs in the primary healer group.

⚔ Melee DPS

  • Stay behind boss and move instantly from Rain of Fire.
  • If Doom targets you, go to Doom camp at 8 seconds remaining.
  • Dedicated Doom squad kills Paladin's add; everyone else stays on boss.

➶ Ranged DPS

  • Stay over 30 yards from boss so Rain of Fire cannot target your group.
  • If Doomed, move to Doom camp at 8 seconds.
  • Stay on boss unless assigned Doomguard duty.

★ Special Assignments

Doom Procedure

  1. Warlock Soulstones Doomed player if available.
  2. At 8 seconds, player runs to Doom camp.
  3. Player dies there.
  4. Paladin picks up Doomguard.
  5. After pickup, player Soulstones back if available.

Doomguard Squad

  • Prot Paladin: primary tank.
  • Healer 3: add healer.
  • Melee 1-3: dedicated killers.
  • Prot Warrior: overlap backup.

Wipe Conditions

  • Doom target dies near main raid.
  • Paladin misses Doomguard pickup.
  • Ranged/healers stand inside 30 yards and receive Rain.
  • Bear enters Howl without HoTs.
  • Player stays in Rain and dies to Unquenchable Flames.

Raid-Leader Calls

“Doom NAME.” “NAME to camp at 8.” “Soulstone NAME.” “Paladin pickup.” “Doom squad kill.” “Howl soon - HoTs Bear.” “Silence.” “Rain - MOVE.”
BOSS 5

Archimonde

What Happens

  • Prot Warrior is the only planned boss tank; Berserker Rage is the preferred answer to every Fear.
  • Every player must have Tears of the Goddess and use it shortly before landing after Air Burst.
  • Deaths create Soul Charges and can cascade into a wipe; survival always beats damage.

🛡 Tanks

Prot Warrior - Permanent Main Tank

  • Tank Archimonde at the boss marker and face away.
  • Never leave melee range or Finger of Death begins.
  • Track Fear and use Berserker Rage for every cast.
  • Do not drag boss around Doomfire; use only minimal lateral adjustment if absolutely required.

Bear - DPS / Utility

  • No planned tank rotation. Do not compete for threat.

Prot Paladin - DPS / Utility

  • No planned tank rotation. Divine Shield is an emergency personal survival tool.

✚ Healers

  • Healers 1-3: Warrior from separated positions.
  • Remaining healers: one per five-player sector.
  • Stay in Tremor and decurse coverage.
  • Grip of the Legion must be decursed immediately.
  • If Air Burst targets you, separate first, then use Tears shortly before landing.

⚔ Melee DPS

  • Spread behind boss rather than one tight stack.
  • When Doomfire approaches melee, completely disengage.
  • Stay in Tremor range.
  • Air Burst target moves away from others and uses Tears before landing.

➶ Ranged DPS

  • Operate in assigned five-player sectors around a Shaman + decurser.
  • Keep enough spacing that Air Burst does not hit neighbors within 13 yards.
  • If Doomfire enters your sector, the whole sector moves into open space without crossing another group.
  • Air Burst target separates, then uses Tears shortly before landing.

★ Special Assignments

Mandatory Before Pull

  • Every player talks to Tyrande and obtains Tears of the Goddess. No Tears = no pull.

Five-Player Sectors

  • Each sector gets 1 Shaman for Tremor whenever roster permits.
  • Each sector gets 1 Mage or Druid assigned to Grip of the Legion decurse.

Air Burst

  1. Target runs away from nearby players.
  2. Air Burst launches target.
  3. Do not use Tears at the top.
  4. Use Tears a few seconds before landing.

Fear

  • Warrior MT: Berserker Rage every Fear.
  • Groups: Tremor Totem primary; PvP trinket/Fear Ward personal backup.

10%

  • Protection of Elune makes the raid invulnerable; until it is active, continue executing normally.

Wipe Conditions

  • Warrior feared out of melee → Finger of Death.
  • Air Burst death from missing/mistimed Tears.
  • Air Burst launches multiple players.
  • Grip stays up for multiple ticks.
  • Fear sends players through Doomfire.
  • One death triggers a Soul Charge cascade.

Raid-Leader Calls

“Tears check - NO TEARS, NO PULL.” “Fear in 5 - Tremors.” “Warrior Berserker Rage.” “Grip NAME - DECURSE.” “Air Burst NAME - CLEAR OUT.” “Tears before landing.” “Doomfire GROUP X - MOVE.” “Survival over DPS.” “10 percent - keep executing.”
