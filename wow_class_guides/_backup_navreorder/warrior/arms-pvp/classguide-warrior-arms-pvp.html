WoW Classic Anniversary TBC - Arms Warrior PvP / Arena Guide

Your fast track to a competitive Arms Warrior in WoW Classic Anniversary (TBC) arena. Stat priority, resilience gear ranked by Arena Season (S1-S4), the deep-Arms Mortal Strike talent build, the macros that make stance-dancing playable, and the burst-plus-CC that turns pressure into kills, with the best 2v2 and 3v3 comps. Every gear pick is ranked from the major PvP guides and checked against live item stats. Hover any item, ability, or set for full Wowhead tooltips.

Overview

Arms is the premier melee in TBC arena. Its identity is relentless pressure plus healing denial: Mortal Strike is a hard-hitting instant that also applies a 50% healing reduction for 10 seconds, so every global you land on a target makes them cheaper to kill. Pair that with Hamstring to lock a target in place, Spell Reflection to punish casters, and Death Wish burst windows, and you have a class that dictates the pace of nearly every match.

Caps first: before you optimize damage, get two things right. (1) Reach roughly 5% Hit so you never miss a Mortal Strike or Hamstring at the wrong moment, a single miss can lose a game. (2) Stack Resilience from your PvP gear, it flatly reduces the chance you are crit and the damage those crits deal, which is why you wear the arena Battlegear over raid gear even when raid pieces show bigger numbers.

Stat priority (PvP): ~5% Hit → Resilience → Stamina → Strength / Attack Power → Critical Strike → a little more Hit / Expertise. Resilience and survivability come first because dead warriors deal no damage; once you are tanky and can reliably land abilities, Strength (1 Str = 2 AP) and Crit turn pressure into kills. Sources genuinely disagree on the exact order of Stamina vs Crit, so treat this as a blend: never sacrifice Resilience or the 5% Hit floor for a shinier damage stat.

Wear PvP gear in arena. A full raid set often shows more Strength and Crit, but with no Resilience you get globalled by any real burst. The arena Battlegear sets below carry Resilience on every piece plus a 2-piece and 4-piece bonus built for warriors. Raid pieces only sneak in where they beat the PvP option and the lost Resilience is acceptable (a couple of high-value rings, cloaks, and the crafted stun weapons).

Rotation & Burst

There is no fixed rotation in arena, only a priority list bent around what the enemy team is doing. The through-line: keep Mortal Strike and Hamstring on the kill target at all times, feed rage into damage, and save your gap-closers and burst for when the enemy healer is controlled.

Damage Priority

When you are on target and have rage, the general order of operations is:

  • Charge / Intercept to reach the target (open with Charge, save Intercept's stun).
  • Hamstring immediately, and keep it up. A kited warrior does nothing; "Hamstring is life."
  • Mortal Strike on cooldown for damage and the 50% healing debuff.
  • Whirlwind as a second instant (also hits multiple enemies if they clump).
  • Execute spam once the target drops below 20% HP, this is where warriors close games.
  • Overpower when the target dodges (can't be dodged or parried, ignores block), your main tool into Rogues.
  • Dump excess rage into Heroic Strike, and keep Rend up on Rogues/Druids to stop them re-stealthing.

Burst / Kill Window

Warriors kill in windows, not with a steady trickle. A window opens when the enemy healer is out of the picture, in CC, out of line-of-sight, feared, or too low on mana. When it does:

  • Intercept the kill target for the 3-second stun (or use it to reach/stun the healer).
  • Pop Death Wish (+20% damage, and Fear immunity for its duration) together with your trinkets, and Recklessness (100% crit for the next swings) if the situation is safe.
  • Chain Mortal Strike → Whirlwind → Execute under the stacked cooldowns.
  • If your partner supplies burst amplifiers (Shaman Bloodlust / Windfury, Druid Faerie Fire), align your window with theirs.

Recklessness is a commitment. It makes you crit for 100% but also take extra damage and it is Berserker-locked. Use it when you are the one applying pressure and reasonably safe, not while being trained by a double-melee team. When in doubt, Death Wish + trinket is the safer burst.

CC, Interrupts & Kiting

  • Snares: keep Hamstring on your target; use Piercing Howl when a target is just out of melee range or several enemies are grouped (AoE snare, no target needed).
  • Interrupts: Pummel (Berserker Stance, 4s lockout) via a focus macro to shut down a key heal or CC. Only kick the casts that matter. Shield Bash (Defensive Stance) is the backup interrupt when you are shielded up.
  • Disarm: Disarm (Defensive Stance) neutralizes enemy warriors, rogues, and hunters; use it from behind so it cannot be dodged/parried.
  • Fear immunity: Berserker Rage breaks an active Fear and grants immunity for its duration; pop it before a Warlock/Priest Fear lands. Death Wish also grants Fear immunity.
  • Peels: Intervene to jump to a teammate and eat a stun/hit for them, or reposition. Intimidating Shout (Fear) buys space or sets up a swap.

Stance Dancing

Arms is a stance-dancing spec: most of your kit lives in different stances, and macros do the swapping for you (see the Macros section).

  • Berserker Stance is home base: Intercept, Pummel, Berserker Rage, Whirlwind, Recklessness.
  • Battle Stance for Charge and Overpower.
  • Defensive Stance for Spell Reflection, Disarm, Shield Bash, and to soak burst (10% less damage taken).

Tactical Mastery lets you keep rage when you swap stances so you can immediately Disarm or Spell Reflect after switching. Even so, dump a big rage pool into an ability before swapping to Battle Stance for Overpower, or you lose that rage.

Cooldowns & Defensives

  • Offensive: Death Wish (+20% dmg, Fear immunity), Recklessness (100% crit), your on-use trinket (e.g. Bloodlust Brooch).
  • Defensive: drop to Defensive Stance when focused; Last Stand (if specced) for +30% max HP when low; Shield Wall (needs a shield equipped) for a 75% damage cut, save it for lethal burst or to survive to Execute range.
  • Trinket: your PvP Medallion of the Horde (or Alliance) breaks a stun/fear/poly. Do not waste it on the first CC; save it for the CC that would kill you or lose the game.

Rage Management

Rage is your resource and your tempo. Open with Bloodrage for an instant pool, and remember that Resilience-heavy enemies take less of your damage, so rage from dealing damage is lower in PvP than PvE (this is why Endless Rage and being crit-fed via Enrage matter). Don't sit on a full rage bar: dump into Heroic Strike so a stance swap or a disarm doesn't waste it, and keep enough banked to Hamstring and Pummel on demand.

Talents

The PvP build is a deep-Arms Mortal Strike build: enough Arms for Mortal Strike, Impale, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization and your weapon specialization, then a Fury dip for Death Wish, Cruelty and rage/enrage talents. The grid below shows a legal, standard 33/28/0 Arms core; the box under it lists the swaps most PvP warriors make.

Key PvP talents and why:

  • Mortal Strike (Arms): the whole reason to play the spec, a hard instant plus 50% healing reduction.
  • Impale (Arms): +20% crit damage on Mortal Strike, Overpower, and Execute, your burst multiplier.
  • Weapon Specialization (Arms): 5 points in Mace (stun proc, the arena meta), Sword, or Poleaxe to match your weapon.
  • Second Wind (Arms): regenerate rage and health whenever you are stunned or immobilized, huge into Rogues and Mages.
  • Improved Hamstring (Arms): a chance to root the target on Hamstring, a game-winning kiting tool.
  • Improved Intercept (Arms): shorter Intercept cooldown = more stuns and more mobility.
  • Death Wish (Fury): +20% damage burst cooldown that also makes you immune to Fear.

Macros

Arms is unplayable at a high level without macros, they collapse stance-dance sequences into a single button so you can Charge, Intercept, Spell Reflect, and interrupt without fumbling stance keys. Copy these into /macro, and replace the bracketed weapon/shield names with your actual item names. These are pulled from the standard TBC warrior PvP macro sets.

Charge / Intercept (gap closers)

One button for both gap closers: out of combat it swaps to Battle Stance and Charges; in combat it swaps to Berserker Stance and Intercepts. The single most important Arms PvP macro.

#showtooltip /cast [nocombat] Charge; Intercept /cast [nocombat] Battle Stance; Berserker Stance

Simple stance-locked versions if you prefer separate buttons:

#showtooltip Charge /cast Battle Stance /cast Charge
#showtooltip Intercept /cast Berserker Stance /cast Intercept

Spell Reflect & Weapon Swaps

Drop to Defensive Stance, equip a one-hander plus shield, and reflect the incoming spell in one press. This turns Polymorph, Fear, and a Mage's burst back on the caster. Replace the names with your 1H and shield.

#showtooltip Spell Reflection /stopcasting /cast Defensive Stance /equipslot 16 Gladiator's Bonegrinder /equipslot 17 Gladiator's Shield Wall /cast Spell Reflection

Swap back to your two-hander afterward (stopcasting cancels a queued Heroic Strike so the swap is instant):

#showtooltip /stopcasting /cast Berserker Stance /equipslot 16 Brutal Gladiator's Bonegrinder

Keep a 1H + shield in your bags (a Brutal Gladiator's Bonegrinder is a two-hander, so carry a one-hand arena weapon and the arena shield like Gladiator's Shield Wall). Spell Reflect and Shield Bash both require the shield equipped, the macro handles the swap for you.

Focus & Interrupt

Interrupt or snare the enemy healer/caster (set as your focus) without leaving your kill target:

#showtooltip Pummel /cast [target=focus,exists,nodead] Pummel; Pummel
#showtooltip Hamstring /cast [target=focus] Hamstring

Mouseover Intervene to peel for a teammate instantly:

#showtooltip Intervene /cast [target=mouseover,exists,nodead] Intervene; [help] Intervene; Intervene

Burst & Utility

Line up both trinkets (slots 13/14) with Death Wish for a burst window:

#showtooltip Death Wish /use 13 /use 14 /cast Death Wish

Recklessness is Berserker-locked, so swap first:

#showtooltip Recklessness /cast Berserker Stance /cast Recklessness

Break or pre-empt a Fear (swaps to Berserker Stance and pops Berserker Rage):

#showtooltip Berserker Rage /cast Berserker Stance /cast Berserker Rage

Overpower after a dodge (Battle Stance), the main tool into Rogues:

#showtooltip Overpower /cast Battle Stance /cast Overpower

Gems

PvP gemming balances survival and damage. There is no Resilience gem in TBC (Resilience only comes on gear), so gems supply Stamina, Strength, Crit, and the Hit you need to reach 5%. A common approach is Stamina-heavy for survivability with enough Strength/Crit to keep pressure up; hit the 5% Hit floor first if your gear is short.

Arena Comps

Arms is a healer's best friend: it applies constant pressure and healing reduction so your team wins the long game. The strongest setups pair the warrior with a healer who can peel and CC (Druid, Paladin) or a partner who amplifies burst (Shaman). Below are the comps that include an Arms Warrior, with how each one plays.

2v2 Comps

CompTierHow it plays
Warrior + Restoration DruidS (best)The premier 2v2 "WD". You apply nonstop Mortal Strike pressure while the Druid keeps you alive with HoTs and controls the enemy healer with Cyclone. Chain Cyclone into your Intercept stun for kill windows. Excels in long games, don't rush
Warrior + Holy PaladinSNearly unkillable: Blessing of Freedom removes snares/roots so you never get kited, Blessing of Sacrifice and Lay on Hands keep you topped. Grind the enemy down with pressure and win attrition
Warrior + ShamanAWindfury Totem turns your autos and burst window into deletes; the Shaman purges enemy defensives (Ice Block, shields, HoTs) and can Grounding/Tremor your CC. Big upfront damage, call Bloodlust in kill windows
Warrior + Discipline PriestAThe Priest Mana Burns the enemy healer out of the game while you pressure, and Fear chains control the match. Pain Suppression and Mass Dispel (bubbles, Ice Block) are clutch. Double-Fear kills are the finisher

3v3 Comps

In 3v3 the warrior is the pressure engine in a double-melee cleave or a warrior/caster/healer setup. These are viable Arms setups; the double-melee-plus-Druid variants are the most common.

CompHow it plays
Warrior / Enhancement Shaman / Resto DruidDouble-melee cleave under Druid CC and heals. Windfury + Mortal Strike burst, Cyclone locks the healer. Heavy, sustained pressure
Warrior / Retribution Paladin / Resto DruidDouble melee with Hammer of Justice + Intercept + Cyclone kill chains. Strong burst on a CCed healer
Warrior / Subtlety Rogue / Resto DruidDouble melee with heavy stun-lock kill setups plus Mortal Strike healing reduction and Cyclone
Warrior / Frost Mage / Resto DruidMage roots/CC (Polymorph, Frost Nova) set up your burst windows; you provide MS pressure and peels
Warrior / Affliction Warlock / Resto DruidWarrior pressure + Warlock DoT/Fear/Curse pressure under Druid Cyclone and HoTs
Warrior / Retribution Paladin / Resto ShamanA 2200+ setup: double melee with Shaman totems/heals and Windfury burst

Kill Priority & Win Conditions

  • Keep Mortal Strike up on the kill target at all times, the 50% healing reduction is why your team wins. A missed MS or Hamstring can lose the game, hence the 5% Hit floor.
  • vs Healer + DPS: either train the healer so they cannot cast (they can't heal while eating MS every few seconds), or force healer CC and burst the DPS. Pick based on which healer you face.
  • vs Double DPS: pressure and swap to whoever is low; you usually win the damage race if you can peel one of them with Disarm/Intimidating Shout.
  • Lock out the healer: at high damage, Pummel the healer to secure a kill instead of tunneling the DPS. Intercept → Pummel the healer when the window opens.
  • Execute range dominance: below 20% HP, swap to low targets and Execute before the heals land.

Best Races

Warrior is Horde or Alliance; race matters most for the racials that survive stuns and escape roots. Weapon-type racials also nudge your weapon-specialization choice.
