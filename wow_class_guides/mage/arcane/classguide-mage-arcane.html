Overview

Arcane is the burst-and-burn caster: the highest raw single-target ceiling of the three mage specs, paid for with the most demanding mana management in the game. You stack spell damage, ramp your nuke, and use every mana tool you own (gems, Evocation, potions, runes) to hold that ramp for the whole fight. It is the spec that rewards planning a fight in advance rather than reacting to procs. Like every mage you bring Arcane Brilliance, sheep CC, and conjured food, water, and mana gems to the raid.

1. Hit the spell hit cap first. Against a level 73 raid boss you need 16% spell hit (202 hit rating) from gear alone. Talents and raid debuffs pay part of that bill, so in a normal raid setup the gear requirement drops to roughly 76 hit rating. Until you are capped, spell hit is worth more than any other stat: a resisted cast is 100% of its damage lost. Confirm your exact number against your own talent points and whether your raid brings the spell-hit debuff.

2. Then follow the stat priority: Spell Hit (to cap) > Spell Damage > Spell Crit > Spell Haste > Intellect. Intellect is worth more to Arcane than to the other mage specs because the spec's talents scale damage and mana off your mana pool, but it never comes before capping hit.

Talents

The raid build is 40/0/21, 40 points in Arcane and a 21-point dip into Frost. Arcane is one of the few TBC specs where the off-tree points buy almost nothing directly: every Frost talent here except two only affects Frost and Fire spells, which you never cast. You spend those 21 points purely to reach Icy Veins and Cold Snap. Open the Wowhead TBC talent calculator to build and export it.

The talents that carry the spec:
Mind Mastery (5/5): turns 25% of your Intellect into spell damage. Combined with the Spellfire 3-set, Intellect becomes your best stat by a wide margin, which is why Arcane gems and enchants look different from every other caster.
Arcane Concentration (5/5) + Arcane Potency (3/3): Clearcasting procs come free, and the next spell after one crits 20% more often. This pairing is most of your crit budget.
Presence of Mind + Arcane Power: your burst window. Cast them together, not separately.
Icy Veins + Cold Snap: 20% casting speed, and Cold Snap resets it, so you get two Icy Veins per fight. This is the entire reason the build dips 21 points into Frost.
Arcane Meditation (3/3) + Arcane Mind (5/5): the mana half of the build. Arcane burns mana faster than any other caster, so these are not optional.

Do not misread the Frost points. Elemental Precision, Frost Channeling, Ice Shards, Piercing Ice and Improved Frostbolt all read like damage or mana talents, but every one of them is worded "your Frost spells" or "Frost and Fire spells". None of them touch Arcane Blast or Arcane Missiles. They are a 17-point tax you pay to unlock Icy Veins and Cold Snap, nothing more. In particular, Elemental Precision does not give you Arcane spell hit, so do not count it toward your hit cap.

Rotation & DPS

Arcane's rotation is a mana curve, not a button sequence: you ramp your main nuke as far as your mana bar allows, drop back to a cheaper filler before you bottom out, and refill with every mana cooldown you have. How far you ramp depends on fight length, your mana pool, and your raid's mana support.

Questing Strategy

Quests reward caster gear, gold, rep, and XP all at once, but only if you batch them. Pick up everything at a hub before you start killing, and most slots in your pre-raid set come from solo questing.

Universal rule: when you reach a quest hub, walk every NPC with a yellow ! before doing anything. Leave town with a full log and a loop that finishes several quests at once. The gear below is a low-effort head start before you grind heroics and badges.

Reputation

Reputation unlocks heroic dungeons, your head and shoulder enchants, and key items. Grind these early to bring your gear online.

Caster Priority Reputations

  • The Sha'tar (Revered) for Glyph of Power, your head enchant. This is the single highest-value reputation for a fresh Arcane mage.
  • Keepers of Time, Lower City, Cenarion Expedition, Honor Hold / Thrallmar (Revered) for their heroic keys.
  • The Violet Eye (Karazhan) for Violet Signet of the Archmage, a top caster ring; wear the lower ranks while grinding.
  • The Consortium and The Scale of the Sands (later) for rings and trinkets.

Aldor vs Scryers

Pick one Shattrath faction (mutually exclusive). For an Arcane Mage it mainly sets your shoulder enchant:

  • Aldor: Greater Inscription of Discipline (+18 spell dmg, +10 crit), the raw-damage option.
  • Scryers: Greater Inscription of the Orb (+12 spell dmg, +15 crit), trades some damage for crit.

Both are close; pick whichever matches your guild, trinket plans, and what your other casters are running.

Phase 1 - Karazhan / Gruul's Lair / Magtheridon's Lair

Phase 1 opens Karazhan, Gruul's Lair and Magtheridon's Lair, and brings the Tier 4 mage set, Aldor Regalia. The defining choice of the phase is not tier at all: the crafted Spellfire three-piece converts your Intellect straight into spell damage, and Arcane stacks more Intellect than any other caster, so it beats chasing the full tier set. Expect a mix of crafted, tier, and Karazhan drops, with Prince Malchezaar and Maiden of Virtue carrying more slots than any other bosses.

Set bonuses that actually matter:
Spellfire (3-set: Robe + Gloves + Belt): increases spell damage by up to 7% of your total Intellect. This is the phase's biggest single gain for Arcane and the reason most of your chest, hands and waist slots are crafted rather than tier.
Aldor Regalia (T4) 2-set: uninterruptible Fireball and Frostbolt. Arcane casts neither, so treat the 2-set as worthless and judge tier pieces purely on their own stats.
Aldor Regalia (T4) 4-set: cuts 24 seconds off Presence of Mind. That one is genuinely good, but reaching it means dropping Spellfire, so it is a Phase 2 goal rather than a Phase 1 target.

Raid Buffs & Debuffs

As a caster your damage scales hard with the right party. Build a caster group and make sure the spell-damage debuffs land on the boss.

Class Buffs That Benefit an Arcane Mage

ClassBuffEffect
MageArcane Brilliance+int to the raid (you bring this)
PaladinGreater Blessing of Wisdommana regen, worth more to Arcane than to any other caster
PaladinGreater Blessing of Kings+10% all stats
PriestPrayer of Spirit+spirit (regen + some spell power)
ShamanWrath of Air Totem+101 spell damage to the party
ShamanBloodlust / Heroism+30% haste for 40 sec

Debuffs to Keep on the Boss

  • Curse of the Elements (Warlock), +10% magic damage taken, the big one
  • Misery (Shadow Priest), +3% spell hit and damage taken; it is the debuff that lets you drop gear hit and re-spend those item budgets on spell damage

Note the difference from Frost: the stacking frost crit debuff does nothing for your damage, so an Arcane Mage's party value comes from the generic magic-damage and spell-hit debuffs above, not from a school-specific one.
